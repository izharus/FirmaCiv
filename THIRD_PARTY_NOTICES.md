The roof shape implementation in
src/main/java/com/alekiponi/firmaciv/common/block/SquaredAngleBlock.java and
the three assets/firmaciv/models/block/roofing*.json parent models are adapted
from alekiponi's AlekiRoofs 1.0.1-1.20.1, whose distributed mod metadata declares
an MIT license. These are included locally so that an AlekiRoofs runtime
installation is not needed.

The boat variant, construction, multiblock and entity loot data are adapted
from aleki's Nifty Ships 2.0.0 (dev/1.21), by Hyper Dash/alekiponi.
The anchor item model overrides its missing particle texture.
